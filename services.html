Services

What we do.And how we leave it.

We turn up, do the job and clean the area before we go. Mowed lawns get edged and the paths blown down. Cut material gets cleared. No prices on this page because every yard is different, so quotes are free and no obligation.

Lawns

  • Lawn mowing
  • Edge trimming
  • Ride-on and acreage mowing
  • Weed spraying
  • Grass removal

Regular or one off mowing, with the edges cut and the paths and driveway blown down after. For bigger blocks we use ride-on mowing. Weeds get sprayed and unwanted grass can be taken out.

Hedges and trees

  • Hedge trimming
  • Tree trimming
  • Branch lopping

Hedges cut back to shape and branches lopped where they hang over paths, fences or the yard. Everything cut gets cleared away.

Yard clean ups

  • Yard tidying
  • Garden clean ups
  • Plant removal
  • Rubbish removal and green waste

For yards that have got away from you. We clear the overgrowth, take out unwanted plants, remove the rubbish and green waste, and leave the area clean.

Pressure washing and mulching

  • Pressure washing
  • Mulching

Pressure washing for hard surfaces around the yard, and mulch to finish off garden beds.
